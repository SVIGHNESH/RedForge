# T11.05 Viva ledger and demonstration rehearsals

**Item:** M11, success criterion 6 | **Milestone:** ongoing from Week 3, final in Week 14 | **Week:** 3 to 14
**Suggested owner:** secondaries | **Reviewer:** Vighnesh
**Effort:** 30 minutes per week, then two 1-hour rehearsals
**Depends on:** nothing to start
**Unblocks:** viva

## Steps

1. Create `docs/VIVA-LEDGER.md` in Week 3 with the eight questions from IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN's ledger section. For each: the answer in three sentences, the evidence (test name, table, decision entry), and two member names.
2. At each weekly sync, after the secondary presents the randomly chosen module, update the ledger for questions touching that module.
3. Add questions as they come up in reviews (for example "why eventfd", "why not fork", "why no replication backlog").
4. Week 14: rehearse the Proposal Section 13 demo script twice end to end on the Docker Compose setup, with the secondaries answering. Time it. Fix anything that needed a workaround.
5. Final check: every question has two names, and each named person answers it cold in the second rehearsal.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Ledger complete with two names per question.
- [ ] Both rehearsals done, the demo runs in under 10 minutes from a clean start.
